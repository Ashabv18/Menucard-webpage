In plain language

What exactly are you buying?

A registration is a legal asset you own — like a deed for a house. Without it, you must prove in court that you used the name first. With it, they must prove otherwise.

India is first-to-file: whoever files first usually wins, even if you used the name earlier.

The decision in front of you

What happens if you don't register

Most business owners discover the value of a trademark on the day they need it — and that is usually too late.

Without a registered trademark

  • Anyone can legally use a name similar to yours in their own region
  • A third party may file the same name before you — then you are the infringer
  • Marketplaces and app stores reject takedown requests without proof of registration
  • Banks and investors discount an unregistered brand when valuing the company
  • Cannot license, franchise or sell the brand as a separate asset
  • Customs cannot stop counterfeit imports of your brand

With a registered trademark

  • Exclusive right to use the ® symbol — a visible deterrent on every package
  • Legal basis for injunctions, damages and seizure of infringing goods
  • Valid across all of India for 10 years from the filing date, renewable forever
  • Brand becomes a balance-sheet asset you can license or sell
  • Faster approvals on Amazon, Flipkart, Google and Meta brand programmes
  • Strong footing for due diligence, funding rounds and MSME registrations

You may use the ™ symbol as soon as the application is filed. The ® symbol can only be used after the registration certificate is issued.

Questions clients ask us

Frequently asked questions

Can I start using the trademark symbol immediately after filing?

Yes. You may use the ™ symbol from the date your application is filed — you do not have to wait for registration. The ® symbol can only be used after the registration certificate is issued.

How long does registration actually take?

It varies, because the pace is set by the Trade Marks Registry's examination queue rather than by how quickly the application is filed. Once accepted, the four-month publication window is fixed by law and cannot be shortened. We give you a realistic expectation for your specific class rather than a promised date.

What if my application gets an objection? Is it rejected?

No. An objection is not a refusal — it is the examiner asking you to answer specific concerns, usually that the mark is descriptive (Section 9) or similar to an existing mark (Section 11). We file a written reply, and a hearing follows only if the written reply is not sufficient. Many objections are cleared at the reply stage.

What happens if I miss the deadline to reply to an objection?

The reply is due within one month of receiving the Examination Report. If nothing is filed, the application can be treated as abandoned and you would have to start again with a new filing date — losing your priority. This is exactly why deadline tracking matters more than the filing itself.

Do I need to register in more than one class?

Often, yes. A clothing brand typically needs Class 25 for the garments and Class 35 for its retail and online store. A software product usually needs Class 9 for the downloadable app and Class 42 for the service. We map your business to the right classes during the free search — filing in too few classes is a common and costly mistake.

I have been using my brand name for years. Am I already protected?

Prior use gives you some rights, but India operates on a first-to-file basis — so a third party who files the same mark before you can put you in a difficult position. Registration converts informal use into a documented statutory right that is far easier to enforce.

Can a descriptive name be registered?

Names that simply describe the product — like "Fresh Milk" for dairy — are usually refused for lack of distinctiveness. Invented or unusual names register most reliably. If your preferred name is descriptive, we will tell you before you spend anything and help you find a stronger alternative.

Does my Indian registration protect me outside India?

No — trademark rights are territorial. An Indian registration protects you only within India. For other countries we file either directly in each country or through a single Madrid Protocol application covering multiple member countries.

How long is the registration valid?

Ten years from the date of filing (not from the date of the certificate), and it can be renewed indefinitely for successive ten-year periods using Form TM-R. We diarise the renewal for you.

What is the difference between a word mark and a logo mark?

A word mark protects the name itself, in any font, colour or style — it is the broader and more valuable registration. A logo or device mark protects the specific graphic. Many businesses file both, since a competitor could otherwise use your name in a different design.

Can a trademark be transferred or licensed to someone else?

Yes. A registered trademark is an intangible asset that can be assigned, licensed or used as security. We prepare and record the assignment or licence with the Registry so the transfer is legally effective.

My mark was registered years ago and I never renewed it. Is it lost?

Not necessarily. There is a restoration route available for a limited period after expiry. Send us the application number and we will check the current status and tell you whether it can be restored or whether a fresh filing is required.
